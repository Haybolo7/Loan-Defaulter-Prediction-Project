Production Pipeline & Architecture

Four-stage end-to-end framework built for low-latency credit risk analysis

1 - Data Audit & Collection

Consolidated three years of lending data and integrated alternative financial sources like payment histories for deeper risk insights.

2 - Model Development

Trained Random Forest & Stacking Classifiers with feature engineering and SMOTE oversampling to maximize predictive precision.

3 - System Integration

Seamlessly connected loan application webhooks to internal risk engines for instantaneous sub-second decision making.

4 - Testing & Optimization

Continuous monitoring of borrower risk segments and live feedback loops to refine risk boundaries and prevent capital loss.
